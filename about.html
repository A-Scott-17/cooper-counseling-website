Meet your therapist

Patricia Cooper,
LCPC, LPC

A collaborative, evidence-based approach to OCD and anxiety treatment.

Hi, I’m Tricia. I am a mental health counselor who specializes in treating OCD and anxiety disorders. I have practiced as a licensed therapist since 2019 and am licensed in Montana, Alaska, and South Carolina.

My work begins with listening carefully to what you are living with and what you want your life to hold. Together, we develop an individualized plan that is practical, respectful, and rooted in your goals.

I use cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) and exposure and response prevention (ERP), evidence-based approaches that help clients build a new relationship with fear, intrusive thoughts, and uncertainty. I also bring experience in assessment, diagnosis, and creating treatment plans for people from varied backgrounds.

I stay engaged with current research and ongoing professional learning in OCD and anxiety treatment. My hope is that you leave therapy with useful tools, greater confidence, and more room to live a fulfilling life.

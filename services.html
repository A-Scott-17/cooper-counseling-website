OCD treatment

Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)

ERP is a widely used, evidence-based treatment for obsessive-compulsive disorder. It helps people gradually approach feared thoughts, situations, or objects while practicing a different response to compulsions and rituals.

Rather than trying to eliminate every uncomfortable thought or feeling, ERP helps you build the ability to tolerate uncertainty and distress without getting pulled into the OCD cycle. Over time, this can create more freedom, improved functioning, and a greater sense of control.

Anxiety treatment

Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

CBT focuses on the relationship between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors. We identify patterns that keep anxiety going, test assumptions with care, and build practical strategies that support the life you want to live.

For OCD, CBT often includes ERP. For other anxiety concerns, it may include learning to face avoidance, respond to worry in new ways, and strengthen your capacity to handle difficult internal experiences.

Appointments are typically 45–60 minutes and may be offered up to twice weekly when clinically appropriate. Cooper Counseling currently offers telehealth appointments only.
